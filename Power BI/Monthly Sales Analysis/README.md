#### 1. Overall Sales Performance:

* Total Orders: 319 (for historical data) and 86 (for the latest data).
* Revenue: $34K for the latest data.
* Total Quantity Sold: 813 units (historical) and 214 units (latest).
* Unique Customers: 319 customers (historical) and 86 customers (latest).

#### 2. Top Performing Product Categories:

* Beauty: Dominates in both revenue and unit sales with a total price per unit of $21.1K (historical) and $5.4K (latest).
* Clothing: The second-best category with $20.1K (historical) and $4.8K (latest).

#### 3. Best-Selling Products:

* Historical Data: "Biker Jacket" (105 units) and "Shampoo" (89 units) are the highest sold products.
* Latest Data: "Biker Jacket" (32 units) and "Earbuds" (26 units) are leading in the latest period.

#### 4. Customer Behavior by Days:

* Historical Trends: Sunday and Monday have the highest order counts (53 and 48, respectively), while Thursday has the least (35).
* Latest Data: Sunday remains the peak day with 21 orders.

#### 5. Revenue by Gender:

* Female customers contribute 62.5% of the total revenue, while male customers contribute 37.5% in the latest data.

#### 6. Interactive Filters:

* Date Range Filter: Allows users to view sales data for custom periods.
* Gender Filter: Enables analysis of sales performance by gender.
* Historical vs. Latest Data: Toggle between cumulative historical data and the latest available data.

#### 7. Data-Driven Decision Insights:

* Focus on Beauty and Clothing categories for promotions as they consistently perform well.
* Invest in marketing efforts targeted for weekend shoppers (Sunday and Monday).
* Explore strategies to improve sales for underperforming weekdays like Thursday.

#### 8. Report Design Highlights:

* Alignment of Visuals: Properly aligned for better readability.
* Filter Placement: Easy-to-access filters ensure a seamless user experience.
* Tooltips: Interactive tooltips provide additional context, such as revenue split by gender.
